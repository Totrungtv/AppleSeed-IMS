# Apple Seed Android Service Center VIP v6.1.1

## Thay doi v6.1.1
- Them nut **ONE-CLICK OPPO VoLTE** trong tab VoLTE / IMS.
- Doi voi OPPO/ColorOS legacy, tool se:
  1. tao backup theo luong hien co;
  2. bat `volte_vt_enabled`;
  3. bat `enhanced_4g_mode_enabled`;
  4. bat `volte_enabled`;
  5. bat `carrier_vt_enabled`;
  6. tu dong reboot thiet bi.
- Khong thay doi IMEI, SIM lock, carrier lock hay firmware modem.
- Giu nut kiem tra IMS/VoLTE de xac minh sau reboot.
