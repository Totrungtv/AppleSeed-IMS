# Apple Seed Android Service Center VIP v6.1.1

Ban tool Android Service Center dang duoc dong bo voi repo AppleSeed-IMS.

## One-click OPPO VoLTE
Trong tab **DICH VU VoLTE / IMS** da them nut **ONE-CLICK OPPO VoLTE**.

Lenh duoc ap dung:
- settings put global volte_vt_enabled 1
- settings put global enhanced_4g_mode_enabled 1
- settings put global volte_enabled 1
- settings put global carrier_vt_enabled 1

Sau khi ap dung tool reboot thiet bi de ROM tai lai cau hinh.
